From confidence in medical images to resilience in speech models, I’m interested in what makes AI useful, interpretable, and dependable.

01
Helmholtz Munich / Institute of AI for Health2026 — Present

Uncertainty Quantification in Neural Cellular Automata

Developing uncertainty quantification frameworks for Neural Cellular Automata (NCA) in biomedical image segmentation. Evaluating model reliability and predictive confidence on benchmark cytological datasets.

Supervisors: Prof. Carsten Marr and Dr. Ario Sadafi.

  • Uncertainty quantification
  • Neural cellular automata
  • Biomedical imaging
Research assistant
02
Sharif University of Technology / RIML Lab2026 — Present

Adversarial Robustness in Speech Foundation Models

Developing and evaluating methods to improve adversarial robustness in speech foundation models and transfer robustness to specialized downstream models.

Supervisor: Prof. Mohammad Hossein Rohban.

  • Adversarial robustness
  • Speech models
  • Transferability
Research assistant
03
University of Tehran / AI for Medicine Lab2025 — 2026

Generative Modeling for MRI-Based Classification

Evaluated generative deep learning models (Diffusion, VAE, LDM) for MRI-based disease classification, using self-supervised representations to mitigate clinical data scarcity.

Supervisors: Prof. Ahmad Kalhor and Dr. Saeed Masoudnia.

  • Diffusion models
  • MRI classification
  • Self-supervised learning
Research assistant
04
TEIAS / Tehran Institute for Advanced Studies2025

LLM-Based Support for Mathematics Learning

Designed an LLM-based chatbot and agent for a study investigating undergraduate mathematics learning. Evaluated learning retention and automated feedback.

Supervisor: Prof. Behnam Bahrak.

  • Large language models
  • Learning retention
  • Automated feedback
Research assistant
